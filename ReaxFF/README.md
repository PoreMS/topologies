# Collection of ReaxFF force fields

fogarty.ffield.reax: Si/O/H
J. C. Fogarty, M. Aktulga, A. Y. Grama, A. C. T. van Duin, and S. A. Pandit, 
"A reactive molecular dynamics simulation of the silica-water interface", 
J. Chem. Phys. 132, 174704 (2010)
https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3407433

zhang.ffield.reax: Al/Si/Na/K/O/H
Y. Zhang, X. Liu, A. C. T. van Duin, X. Lu, and E. J. Meijer, 
"Development and validation of a general-purpose ReaxFF reactive force field for earth material modeling", 
J. Chem. Phys. 160, 094103 (2024)
https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0194486

larsson_2807.ffield.reax: C/H/O/N/S/Si/Na
larsson_3196.ffield.reax: C/H/O/N/S/Si/Na
H. R. Larsson, A. C. T. van Duin, and B. Hartke
"Global optimization of parameters in the reactive force field ReaxFF for SiOH", 
J. Comput. Chem. 34, 2178–2189 (2013)
http://doi.org/10.1002/jcc.23382
